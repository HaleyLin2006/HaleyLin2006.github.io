A few reasons people in research keep a blog, in case you want a starting point:

- **Writing is thinking.** Explaining an idea in plain words shows you quickly whether you understand it.
- **Notes compound.** A derivation you write up carefully once is one you never have to redo from scratch.
- **It is a low-stakes place to be wrong.** A post can be revised next week; a paper cannot.

I expect to write mostly about macroeconomics, the papers I am reading, and the tools I am learning along the way.
